# 0006: Retention through pacing, never hidden rigging

**Date:** 2026-11 · **Status:** Accepted

**Decision:** Copy Block Blast's retention thesis (spacing rewards and difficulty) without manipulative rigging. Difficulty is a smooth, clamped function of rings passed and level; playing well is never punished; ads have a cap and a minimum interval.

**Don't:** secretly alter outcomes. A comeback mechanic (such as the "rogue asteroid" idea) must be visible to the player and needs Eugene's decision first.
