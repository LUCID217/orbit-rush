Orbit Rush is a one-tap arcade game you can play in seconds and chase for weeks.

Your dot swings around a ring. Tap to let go and fly into the next one. Land dead centre for a PERFECT, chain them into combos, and set the screen on fire in FEVER mode.

• One-tap controls: easy to start, satisfying to master
• PERFECT combos, FEVER mode and big, punchy feedback
• Fair, skill-based difficulty that ramps up as you climb
• A free save each run, and instant retry when you miss
• Missions, daily streaks and levels that always move you forward
• Unlock colours, skins, worlds, sounds and effects
• An original soundtrack that builds with your combo
• Plays offline, in portrait, with one thumb

Orbit Rush contains ads. Bonus rewards from ads are always optional. You can remove banner and between-run ads with a one-time purchase.
